# 传画接龙: decisions log

Owner: product-ux. Companion to `docs/chuanhua/spec.md` and `docs/chuanhua/direction.md`. Constitution Art. 4.3: product and technical decisions are made by the team, not asked, and logged here with date, decision, reason and alternatives. The founder may override any entry.

IDs:
- **F-n**: founder decision, relayed by the CTO. This log never creates one.
- **PO-n**: product-owner call (product-ux) under the CTO's delegation. The CTO can reverse any PO call. Founder-only matters (money, credentials, filings, release; Art. 5) are never decided here; they are in the Founder Handoff (spec §18).
