# 比一比: decisions log

Owner: product-ux. Companion to `docs/bibiyi/spec.md` and `docs/bibiyi/direction.md`.
Under constitution Art. 4.3 every product decision is logged here with its date, reason and the alternatives considered. The founder may override any entry. IDs:
- **F-n**: founder decision, relayed by the CTO. This log never creates one.
- **PO-n**: product-owner call by product-ux. The CTO can reverse any PO call. Founder-only matters (credentials, money, filings, uploads, `main`) are never decided here; they are listed in the Founder Handoff (spec §16).

| 2026-10-15 | PO-2 | **Interaction: the top card shows its value, the bottom card is hidden; the player taps hi or lo; on a correct answer the bottom card becomes the next top card.** Cards are stacked vertically. | An anchor value makes the question concrete and learnable in seconds (Art. 3.2) and makes every reveal a lesson. | 2026-10-15 | PO-6 | **Daily deck structure:** 40 questions in segments of 4-8 per measure; weekday themes for questions 1-20 (周一 城市, 周二 山河, 周三 古今, 周四 动物, 周五 天文与工程, 周六 吃喝与距离, 周日 大杂烩), mixed after that. | Themes give each day a flavour and a share-card tag ([KEPT] §4) while the mixed tail keeps long runs varied. | 2026-10-15 | PO-7 | **Pair rules and difficulty bands:** same measure only; min gap per measure by data precision (×1.05 for precise survey/scientific/computed data, ×1.10 for census, rivers, structures, ×1.25 lakes, ×1.30 food, ×1.50 animals; years ≥ 30 and ≥ 2× uncertainty; approx facts 1.5× the min gap); bands narrow by question position. | Near-ties cause "you're wrong!" anger ([KEPT] §4 used 10%, [ALT] 5%). | 2026-10-15 | PO-8 | **Analytic difficulty gate and live daily generation; no seed bank.** A typical-player model gives `p_k` per question; the deck passes if the model median streak is 6-12, P(≥ 20) is 2-15% and P(40) ≤ 0.5%. `genDaily` generates at 23:00 BJT; a failure publishes nothing (`DAILY_UNAVAILABLE`).
