# 群友刻度盘 (kedupan): decisions log

Owner: product-ux. Companion to `docs/kedupan/spec.md` (the "what") and `docs/kedupan/direction.md` (vision, art, risks).

The founder approved building this game (2026-10-15). Under the constitution (Art. 4.3) product decisions are made by the team and logged here with date, decision, reason and alternatives. The founder may override any of them. IDs:
- **D-n**: founder decision (relayed by the CTO; this log never creates one).
- **PD-n**: product decision by product-ux, made within the constitution and the CTO's brief. The CTO can reverse any PD.

Founder-level matters (money, credentials, filings, uploads, release) are never decided here. They are listed in the Founder Handoff (spec §16).

| Date | ID | Decision | Reason | Alternatives considered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-15 | **D-1** | **Build 群友刻度盘 as a complete v1** (slug `kedupan`), on the reworked two-mode design from `research/2026-10-15-new-ideas/kept-ideas.md` §2. | Founder approval, relayed by the CTO. | n/a |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-1** | **Three ways to play, one widget.** (1) **每日刻度** (daily, main mode, no clue-giver): 5 cards a day, everyone places the same 5 prompts, scored against the nation and the group. (2) **出题** (group-clue mode): a host gets a hidden target and gives a clue; the group guesses async. (3) **往期刻度** (archive): any closed day, unranked, unlimited. | The daily removes the host role from the main loop, which is what failed Simple in the shortlist (kept-ideas §2). The archive fixes the weakest score (Addictive 3: "no play-again-now"). The host mode keeps the purest Wavelength-style group dynamic as an optional social engine. | (a) Host mode only (kept-ideas-alt): Simple 3, dead rounds in quiet groups. (b) Daily only: loses the group-as-game moment. (c) Daily plus editor-set target ("guess our number"): you vs an editor is arbitrary and not social. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-2** | **The widget is a straight horizontal ruler (刻度尺), 0-100 internally, not a semicircular dial.** Tap or drag anywhere on the track; ‹ › nudge buttons move 1 step; no needle until the first touch (no anchoring at 50); no numbers shown while placing; confirm with 就这儿. | Not copying Wavelength's trade dress (semicircle, coloured scoring wedge, screen cover). A full-width straight track is the easiest one-thumb target for a 40+ audience and gives the finest resolution on a narrow phone. No default needle avoids lazy "leave it in the middle" answers that would drag every median to 50. | Semicircular dial: closest to the source game's look (IP risk) and harder to hit with a thumb near the edges. Vertical thermometer: less resolution on short screens. Numeric 0-100 input: not a game. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-3** | **One scoring formula everywhere:** card score `s = max(0, 100 − ceil(2.5 × d))`, `d` = absolute distance on the 0-100 track. 0 → 100, 10 → 75, 20 → 50, ≥ 40 → 0. Daily 默契分 = sum of 5 cards (0-500). Card labels by `d`: ≤ 2 心有灵犀, ≤ 6 很同频, ≤ 12 差不多, ≤ 20 有点偏, > 20 想法独特. | One rule learned once works for daily, group and host rounds (Simple). Linear is explainable in one line ("差 1 格扣 2.5 分"). Labels are positive even at the bottom ("想法独特", never "错"). Integers via `ceil` are deterministic on client and server. | Wavelength-style 2/3/4-point wedge: IP-adjacent and coarse. Quadratic or Gaussian curve: harder to explain. Rank-based score: meaningless with few players. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-4** | **National reference = live median with an editor prior.** For each daily card, `R = median(all ranked positions ∪ 10 virtual votes at the item's editor anchor)`. The score is **live** (recomputed from the current histogram whenever shown) and **final at D+1 00:30 BJT** (定分), frozen by `finalizeDay`. | Scores "against the crowd" need a crowd. The 10-vote prior stops the first few players from defining the answer for everyone, and fades to nothing at scale (10 of 1,000 votes). Recomputing for everyone from the same histogram keeps every board fair at every moment. 定分 the next morning is a reason to come back ("昨日定分 421，超过全国 68%"). | (a) Score against players before you only: early players get noise, late players get a better reference, unfair. (b) Editor-set answer: arbitrary and not social. (c) Score only after close: kills the instant reveal, the core payoff. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-5** | **Group scoring.** Your **群内默契分** = Σ cards `s(|p − median of the other members' positions|)` (leave-one-out; needs ≥ 1 other member). **群默契度** (the group stat) = mean over the 5 cards of `max(0, 100 − ceil(2.5 × MAD))`, MAD = mean absolute deviation of members' positions from the group median; shown only with ≥ 3 members who completed the day. Group board ranks 群内默契分. | Leave-one-out stops your own needle from pulling the reference to you in small groups. MAD gives one number per group per day that rises when the group agrees, which is exactly 默契. A 3-member minimum keeps the stat meaningful. | Including yourself in the median: in a 2-person group you'd always score higher than the other player. Group average of members' scores: double-counts and depends on group size. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-6** | **Group membership = anyone who has ever entered the game from a card in that group** (server-decrypted via `joinGroup`, `group_members`, 点将台 pattern). Once a member, all their dailies count for that group, whatever their entry path. The group page opens only from a group-card entry. | It is the only trustworthy group signal (the openGId is decrypted server-side). Counting members' later dailies from home makes the group stat stable without forcing card entry each day. | Use the open data domain's group list to define membership: client-side, tamperable, and the server could not compute group stats. A "my groups" list from home: we have no group names to show, so the list would be unreadable. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-7** | **Host-mode clues contain no user text inside the game.** Two clue types: **选提示词** (default: the host picks one of 6 curated items for that spectrum, with one free 换一批) and **群里说** (the host says the clue in the WeChat chat as a normal message or voice; the card says "提示在群聊里"). So **`msgSecCheck` is not needed and there is no UGC report button**; a no-text "这张卡有问题" content feedback exists instead. | Founder brief: avoid in-game free-text UGC if possible. Presets work for late openers, who can't find a chat message; the chat option keeps free, funny clues where they belong (WeChat's own surface, governed by WeChat). Zero UGC means no moderation pipeline, no 100-calls/day pre-publish quota problem and no extra category qualification risk. | Typed clue ≤ 8 characters through `msgSecCheck` v2 plus report and takedown: adds a moderation pipeline and a whole UGC compliance surface for a small gain. Backlog only. Chat-only clues: late openers are lost. Preset-only: removes the most creative part of the game. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-8** | **Host round rules.** Target: server-drawn integer, uniform in [5, 95], shown only to the host. Reveal: the host taps 揭晓 once ≥ 2 on-time guesses exist, **or** automatically at the 8th guess, **or** automatically 24 h after creation if ≥ 1 guess exists. A round with 0 guesses at 24 h becomes 无人作答. New guesses close 7 days after creation. After the reveal, latecomers may still place a "先猜再看" guess (shown, not counted). Rounds are not locked to one group: anyone with the card can guess. | Ends avoided so every target has room on both sides. Three reveal paths prevent dead rounds while keeping the collective reveal moment (nobody sees the target before it). Auto-reveal at 8 suits typical active groups. Not locking to a group lets a host use single chats too. | Reveal to each guesser instantly: guessers could leak the target in the chat before others answer. Host-chosen target: removes the host's challenge. Group-locked rounds: a forwarded card would just fail. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-9** | **Host-round scoring:** each on-time guess scores `s(|guess − target|)` (PD-3). **本题默契值** = mean of on-time guess scores, rounded. The host's 出题默契 = that 默契值. The closest guesser is shown as 最懂你. | Same formula as the daily (one rule). The host's score rewards a clue the group understood, which is the whole point. | Wedge points (IP-adjacent). Host scored on the best single guess: rewards luck. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-10** | **Archive (往期刻度): every closed day, unranked, unlimited, free.** Scored instantly against that day's frozen final medians. Results kept on the device only. A day you played as the daily opens as a read-only review. | The "play again now" loop the research said was missing (Addictive 3). Uses data we already have. The natural place for interstitials, keeping ads out of the daily and the host mode. | Archive behind ads: a dark pattern for core content. Last 30 days only: needlessly limits replay. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-11** | **Spoiler rule for share cards:** the daily card shows today's prompts, the band block per card and the total, **never needle positions or medians** (people who haven't played would see the answer). Archive and closed days may show positions. Host-round cards before the reveal show only the spectrum, the clue (preset) or "提示在群聊里". | Same idea as 点将台 D4. The prompts are the invitation; the positions are the answer. | Show your needle: spoils the crowd answer and the fun of the reveal. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-12** | **IAA placements.** Rewarded: **加一题** (an unranked bonus card from closed days, ≤ 3 per day), **全国分布** (detailed histograms for every card of one set, once per set), **换一批题目** (host mode, beyond the one free swap; ≤ 3 per round). Interstitial: **only between archive sets** (after every 2nd completed set in a session; not before 3 lifetime completed sets; ≥ 120 s apart; ≤ 6 per day; remote-config caps with clamps). Banner: boards and the archive list only, **config, default off**. No ads in the daily play flow or the host-round flow except the optional rewarded swap. On no-fill, the reward is granted at most once per placement per day (点将台 D6 pattern). | The fit is weak (kept-ideas §5: IAA 2); placing ads where they cannot hurt the daily habit or the group moment is worth more than volume. Every reward is an extra, never the core (the daily, its reveal and the group board are always free). | Rewarded "see the national median": paywalls the payoff, a dark pattern. Interstitial after the daily: hits the most important moment of the day. Ads in host rounds: would make people stop hosting. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-13** | **One opt-in subscribe message "刻度题揭晓了"**, offered to the host after creating a round and to a guesser after guessing, from their own tap only; one message per grant; template ID from remote `app_config` (founder's MP console); hidden when absent. | Directly fights dead rounds (people learn when the reveal happens) without nagging. Same proven pattern as 点将台 PO-10. | Daily reminder: not allowed as a one-time template without a user action each time, and it is pressure. Long-term subscription: not available to games for this use. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-14** | **No updatable share messages in v1.** | The only template is "成员正在加入，当前 {member_count}/{room_limit} 人" ([P0] 5), whose wording does not fit a guessing round. The in-game count does the job. | Use it anyway: confusing copy. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-15** | **No save-to-album in v1.** | The share card covers sharing; album saving adds a privacy interface (相册（仅写入）) and a permission flow for little value in a group game. | Copy 点将台 §8.12: more surface, more device-pass items. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-16** | **Accessibility: large-text and high-contrast modes**, copied from 点将台 §8.10 (PO-12). The three markers (you ▼, nation ◆, group ●) differ by **shape** as well as colour. | Our audience skews 40+. Shape coding makes the reveal readable for colour-blind players. | Colour only: fails colour-blind users. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-17** | **Streak = consecutive published days with a complete daily (all 5 cards).** Days with no published set are skipped. No rewards, no freeze, no reminders. | Same rule as 点将台 PO-6; a complete daily is the unit of habit. | Count any card: inflates streaks with one tap. Streak rewards: login-reward pressure. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-18** | **Content plan:** **≥ 300 original spectra**, each with **≥ 10 prompt items** (≥ 3,000 items), each item with an editor anchor (5-95); 12 categories with quotas; an offline-built schedule of ≥ 365 days with no item ever repeated and no spectrum repeated within 21 days. Items double as host-mode clue presets. | 5 cards a day need 1,825 items for a year; 3,000 gives headroom for rejects, bonus cards and the host mode. Anchors seed the prior (PD-4). | 300 spectra × 6 items: only 1 year of dailies with nothing left for the host mode. Writing a fresh daily each day: an ops burden and a review risk. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-19** | **The content library ships in the package as `.js` modules (`core/content/spectra.v1.js`); the daily set comes from the server** (`daily_sets`, from the shipped schedule, editable in the console). | Mini games can only `require` `.js` files. The library must be local for the archive, host mode and offline display. A server-side daily set lets ops pull a problem card without a release. | Server-only content: the host mode and archive would need the network for every label. Client-only schedule: no way to hot-swap a bad card. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-20** | **"最同频的群友" is computed and drawn only inside the open data domain** (the friend or group member whose 5 positions today are closest to yours, by mean distance), visible only to a viewer who completed the day. | Names exist only in the open data domain; it is a fun, positive comparison about opinions on things, never a rating of a person. | Server-side pairs: the server doesn't know who your friends are. "群里谁最…" style person ratings: banned by our content rules. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-21** | **Friend board ranks the national 默契分; the group page ranks 群内默契分.** Both are server-computed from stored positions (C3 pattern from 点将台): a tampered KV value never earns a rank. | Friends are not one group, so their only shared reference is the nation. In a group, closeness to the group is the point. | One score for both: either loses the group meaning or makes friend boards incoherent. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-22** | **No cross-group ranking or percentile in v1** (no "本群默契度超过全国 80% 的群"). | Needs a nightly job over every group and invites group-vs-group league pressure; the in-group 7-day trend gives enough story. | Cross-group percentile: backlog if KPIs suggest it. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-23** | **Name:** 群友刻度盘 (working title and first choice). Fallbacks for registration: 刻度默契, 群友刻度尺, 同频刻度. Never 波长, 心灵波长 or "Wavelength". | Founder's chosen title. Fallbacks avoid any translation of the source game's name. | n/a |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-24** | **Partial dailies.** Every submitted card counts in the national histogram at once. Only a complete daily (5 cards) ranks on boards and counts for streaks and group stats. A daily started on D may finish until D+1 00:30 BJT; later cards return `LATE_SUBMIT` and the day stays incomplete. | Each card's reveal must update the crowd instantly; boards compare like with like. Grace window mirrors 点将台. | Rank partial days: unequal comparisons. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-25** | **Anti-cheat is light: server-stored positions, one submission per card, rate limits, and an observe-only `fast` flag.** A second account could peek at the national median before the main account plays; this is accepted. | There is nothing to compute client-side and the boards carry no prizes. Server-computed scores already stop forged scores. | Device fingerprinting or bans: privacy cost and false positives for no prize. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-26** | **Code reuse by copy, never by import.** `apps/kedupan/` copies the patterns and code it needs from `apps/dianjiangtai/` (date, rng, store, joinGroup, getBoard hashing, open data domain with SHA-256, track, cleanup, ads, privacy, share, render kit, fake-`wx` harness, sync-core and drift check). Nothing under `apps/kedupan/` requires a path outside it. | Studio rule from the CTO brief; each app deploys alone. | Shared package import: breaks independent deploys and mini game `require` rules. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-27** | **Post-launch KPIs, not gates** (spec §1). | Constitution Art. 4.2: no validation pauses. | Pre-launch group test: not requested by the founder. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-28** | **No interstitial or banner anywhere in the host-round flow** (create, guess, reveal). Only the rewarded 换一批 exists there. | The host mode is the social engine; ads there would make hosting feel costly. | Interstitial after a reveal (kept-ideas-alt): hits the payoff moment. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-29** | **Daily card set rules:** 5 cards from 5 different categories and 5 different spectra; card 1 is always an "easy consensus" item (anchor ≤ 20 or ≥ 80) so first-timers get a satisfying first reveal; at least one "divisive" item (editor tag `split`) per day. | The first reveal teaches the game; a divisive card creates the day's argument ("全国对'一杯奶茶贵不贵'吵翻了"). | Random 5: the first card may be confusing, and some days would have no talking point. |

The first card shows one coach bubble on the ruler. | Learnable in under 30 s (Art. 3.2). | Interactive tutorial round: slower, and the first daily card is the tutorial anyway. |
