# 群友刻度盘 (kedupan): decisions log

Owner: product-ux. Companion to `docs/kedupan/spec.md` (the "what") and `docs/kedupan/direction.md` (vision, art, risks).

The founder approved building this game (2026-10-15). Under the constitution (Art. 4.3) product decisions are made by the team and logged here with date, decision, reason and alternatives. The founder may override any of them. IDs:
- **D-n**: founder decision (relayed by the CTO; this log never creates one).
- **PD-n**: product decision by product-ux, made within the constitution and the CTO's brief. The CTO can reverse any PD.

Founder-level matters (money, credentials, filings, uploads, release) are never decided here. They are listed in the Founder Handoff (spec §16).

| 2026-10-15 | **PD-1** | **Three ways to play, one widget.** (1) **每日刻度** (daily, main mode, no clue-giver): 5 cards a day, everyone places the same 5 prompts, scored against the nation and the group. | 2026-10-15 | **PD-2** | **The widget is a straight horizontal ruler (刻度尺), 0-100 internally, not a semicircular dial.** Tap or drag anywhere on the track; ‹ › nudge buttons move 1 step; no needle until the first touch (no anchoring at 50); no numbers shown while placing; confirm with 就这儿. | Not copying Wavelength's trade dress (semicircle, coloured scoring wedge, screen cover). Vertical thermometer: less resolution on short screens. Numeric 0-100 input: not a game. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-3** | **One scoring formula everywhere:** card score `s = max(0, 100 − ceil(2.5 × d))`, `d` = absolute distance on the 0-100 track. 0 → 100, 10 → 75, 20 → 50, ≥ 40 → 0. Daily 默契分 = sum of 5 cards (0-500). Card labels by `d`: ≤ 2 心有灵犀, ≤ 6 很同频, ≤ 12 差不多, ≤ 20 有点偏, > 20 想法独特. | One rule learned once works for daily, group and host rounds (Simple). Integers via `ceil` are deterministic on client and server. | Wavelength-style 2/3/4-point wedge: IP-adjacent and coarse. Quadratic or Gaussian curve: harder to explain. Rank-based score: meaningless with few players. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-4** | **National reference = live median with an editor prior.** For each daily card, `R = median(all ranked positions ∪ 10 virtual votes at the item's editor anchor)`. The score is **live** (recomputed from the current histogram whenever shown) and **final at D+1 00:30 BJT** (定分), frozen by `finalizeDay`. | Scores "against the crowd" need a crowd. The 10-vote prior stops the first few players from defining the answer for everyone, and fades to nothing at scale (10 of 1,000 votes). Recomputing for everyone from the same histogram keeps every board fair at every moment. 定分 the next morning is a reason to come back ("昨日定分 421，超过全国 68%"). | (a) Score against players before you only: early players get noise, late players get a better reference, unfair. (b) Editor-set answer: arbitrary and not social. (c) Score only after close: kills the instant reveal, the core payoff. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-5** | **Group scoring.** Your **群内默契分** = Σ cards `s(|p − median of the other members' positions|)` (leave-one-out; needs ≥ 1 other member). **群默契度** (the group stat) = mean over the 5 cards of `max(0, 100 − ceil(2.5 × MAD))`, MAD = mean absolute deviation of members' positions from the group median; shown only with ≥ 3 members who completed the day. Group board ranks 群内默契分. | Leave-one-out stops your own needle from pulling the reference to you in small groups. MAD gives one number per group per day that rises when the group agrees, which is exactly 默契. A 3-member minimum keeps the stat meaningful. | Including yourself in the median: in a 2-person group you'd always score higher than the other player. Group average of members' scores: double-counts and depends on group size. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-6** | **Group membership = anyone who has ever entered the game from a card in that group** (server-decrypted via `joinGroup`, `group_members`, 点将台 pattern). Once a member, all their dailies count for that group, whatever their entry path. The group page opens only from a group-card entry. | It is the only trustworthy group signal (the openGId is decrypted server-side). Counting members' later dailies from home makes the group stat stable without forcing card entry each day. | Use the open data domain's group list to define membership: client-side, tamperable, and the server could not compute group stats. A "my groups" list from home: we have no group names to show, so the list would be unreadable. |
| 2026-10-15 | **PD-7** | **Host-mode clues contain no user text inside the game.** Two clue types: **选提示词** (default: the host picks one of 6 curated items for that spectrum, with one free 换一批) and **群里说** (the host says the clue in the WeChat chat as a normal message or voice; the card says "提示在群聊里"). Zero UGC means no moderation pipeline, no 100-calls/day pre-publish quota problem and no extra category qualification risk. | Typed clue ≤ 8 characters through `msgSecCheck` v2 plus report and takedown: adds a moderation pipeline and a whole UGC compliance surface for a small gain. Backlog only. Chat-only clues: late openers are lost. Preset-only: removes the most creative part of the game. **Superseded 2026-10-16 by D-2 and PD-31 to PD-40:** typed clues are in v1 (default tab), 群里说 is removed, and the game now has a moderated UGC surface. |
