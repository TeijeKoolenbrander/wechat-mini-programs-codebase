# 群友刻度盘 account avatar

Original, code-drawn avatar generated by `build.js` (resvg). No third-party IP, no AI art.

**Design.** A yellow paper tape measure across a plum-purple field, with ruler ticks (long tick at the centre), the three game markers sitting on it: tangerine ▼ (you) above, teal ◆ (nation) and plum ● (group) on the tape. Two small sticky notes, pale blue (冷) and pale orange (热), sit under the ends. It is deliberately a ruler, not a semicircular dial, so it cannot be mistaken for Wavelength.

**Palette.** plum #8A5A9A to #5A3069, tape yellow #F2C94C, ink #24211E, you #F07B2C, nation #2A8C8C, group #7D4E8C, paper #FBF6EC, notes #CFE3F5 and #FFD3A8 (direction §6 palette).

**Text and font.** None. Pure code-drawn vector shapes, no font licence involved.

**Legibility at small size.** At 40 px the yellow bar and the orange ▼ read clearly against the purple; the ticks blur into texture and the teal and plum markers are small but still distinguishable. The ▼ alone is the identity. The decorative notes sit inside the circle crop. Checked on `preview.png` (360 px square, circle crop, and 40 px and 100 px on light and dark grounds). All content sits inside the central ~0.85 of the circle, so the circle crop WeChat applies in lists loses nothing.

**Rebuild:** `npm i && node build.js` (Node 18+; `node_modules/` is gitignored). Font only for the 比 glyph in 比一比: Noto Serif SC variable (SIL OFL 1.1), read from `FONT_DIR` (default `C:/Windows/Fonts/NotoSerifSC-VF.ttf`).
