# 语义热冷: decisions log

Owner: product-ux, under the CTO (constitution Article 4.3). Companion to `docs/yuyirelen/spec.md` and `docs/yuyirelen/direction.md`.

IDs:
- **YR-D-n**: founder decision, relayed by the CTO. This log never creates one.
- **PO-n**: product decision made by product-ux within the CTO's brief. The CTO or the founder can reverse any PO decision. Founder-only matters (money, credentials, filings, uploads) are not decided here; they are in the Founder Handoff (spec §19).

| 2026-10-15 | PO-1 | **One daily secret word for everyone** (Beijing date, from server time), unlimited guesses, each guess returns its rank (1 = answer). | The proven Contexto/Semantle core; one shared word makes group comparison fair. | Per-player random words: kills group comparison. Several dailies: dilutes the shared moment. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-2 | **Dictionary-only input.** Only vocabulary words can be guessed. Out-of-vocabulary text is rejected on the device, never sent and never logged. The server re-validates and stores nothing it rejects. | Removes free-text UGC entirely, so no `msgSecCheck` pipeline, no moderation load and no risk of user text reaching others. Makes every displayed word reviewable. | Free text plus `msgSecCheck`: 100 calls per day before publishing, latency per guess, and still lets odd strings into logs. Free text with a local filter only: incomplete and still UGC. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-3 | **Simple-score fix:** 6 date-seeded 热身词 for the first move; 5 联想词 chips after every guess (the guessed word's own top neighbours, answer-independent); native keyboard with dictionary autocomplete; hints offered as one button; 3-line how-to; 3 coach bubbles. | Answers `kept-ideas-alt.md`'s flag that typing fails Simple (scored 2, borderline 3 with a rework). With chips, the whole daily can be played by taps (direction §4: Simple 4). Chips leak nothing because they don't depend on the answer. | (a) Our own in-canvas pinyin keyboard: worse than the system IME, large build. (b) Answer-aware suggestions: leak the answer. (c) Multiple-choice guesses: no longer the genre. (d) Typing only (kept-ideas §1 with autocomplete): still fails one-thumb play for many. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-4 | **Hint ladder:** free hints unlock at steps 10/20/30/40 (word ≤ rank 300, word ≤ 50, first character, word ≤ 10). Counting hints as steps keeps one comparable number. Keeping ads out of ranked play means ads never buy rank (same rule as 点将台's no ad re-spins in the daily). | kept-ideas: ≤ 1 ad hint in the daily with the hint count shown: ads still buy rank. kept-ideas-alt: ad hints count +5 guesses: an extra rule to explain, and still an ad touching ranked play. Hints free of step cost: makes the step count less meaningful. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-5 | **Score = steps.** Boards rank solvers by steps, then hints, then finish time. In-progress ranked players appear as a "正在猜" race section (steps and best rank, no words); given-up players last. Boards carry no words, so no spoiler lock is needed. | One number, easy to compare. The race rows make "the group races the same word" visible without a co-op mode. | Rank by time: punishes thoughtful players and the 40+ audience. Hints-first lexicographic order: a 60-step no-hint run would beat a 12-step one-hint run, which feels wrong. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-6 | **Embedding model: BAAI `bge-large-zh-v1.5`** (MIT; the official model card says "The released models can be used for commercial purposes free of charge"; checked 2026-10-15). **Fallback and bake-off rival: `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B`** (Apache-2.0; official card checked 2026-10-15). A blind bake-off on 40 candidate answers decides; the switch needs ≥ 0.3 higher mean rating, or a tie within 0.3 plus ≥ 10 points lower character-overlap ratio. Offline only. | Both licences are confirmed for commercial use from primary sources. bge-zh is Chinese-specific; Qwen3 is a strong multilingual alternative (C-MTEB 66.33). The bake-off checks what matters (do neighbours *feel* fair). | Tencent AI Lab word vectors: best-known static Chinese vectors, but the official page states no licence (UNVERIFIED), so rejected until one is published. fastText cc.zh: CC BY-SA share-alike could reach our rank tables. text2vec, Chinese-Word-Vectors: licences not confirmed. Any runtime model: forbidden by the brief and adds cost and AIGC questions. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-7 | **Vocabulary source: jieba `dict.txt` (MIT)**, filtered to 1-4 characters, 3,500 common characters, common-noun/verb/adjective/time/place-word POS, top frequency; no person, place, organisation or other proper names; no 成语 or set phrases. Target V = 30,000 (20,000-40,000 allowed). Optional THUOCL 食物/动物 (MIT, "可用于研究与商业") to fill coverage gaps. | jieba is MIT and carries frequency and POS, which the filters need. Removing proper nouns removes most political, brand and celebrity risk at one stroke. Idioms rank unfairly in embeddings. | CC-CEDICT, wordfreq data (share-alike); SUBTLEX-CH (academic terms); HSK lists (copyright unclear); a bigger 50-100k vocabulary (kept-ideas-alt): more rare words means more review and more "unfair" ranks. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-8 | **Server-side ranks.** Each answer's full rank array (uint16 per vocabulary word, ~60 KB) lives in `answer_bank`; `guess` maps the word with the puzzle's vocabulary and looks the rank up, caching the day's array per warm instance. The client sends the word string, never sees the answer, its ranks or the bank. | Server-authoritative play (Article 9.4) and no answer leak. Sending the string (not an index) tolerates vocabulary-version skew between client and puzzle. | Client-side rank table: trivially reveals the answer. Precomputed top-1000 only ([SWEEP]): words outside get no rank, which feels broken; the full array is cheap. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-8a | **Sensitive filter in four layers:** public `konsheng/Sensitive-lexicon` (MIT per third-party write-ups; **licence UNVERIFIED**, used only as a private build input and never shipped), our own category blocklist, an identity-word watchlist whose chips are hand-reviewed, and a full read-through by two reviewers plus the founder's spot-check. Post-launch: a one-off `msgSecCheck` sweep. | Defence in depth, with the parts we control (own list, review) not depending on an unverified licence. | Rely on one public list: incomplete and licence-dependent. `msgSecCheck` before launch: only 100 calls per day until published. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-9 | **Answer rules and gate:** 2-3 character, frequency rank ≤ 8,000, neutral, unambiguous words; bot gate (chip-walker median 10-30, p90 ≤ 45; typist median ≤ 60; ≥ 95% solved within 80 steps); per-answer top-100 review; up to 3 reviewed exact synonyms that also count as solving. Difficulty tags drive a schedule (no two `hard` days in a row, ≤ 2 per week, no repeated category on consecutive days). | Fairness is the product: the top-100 is shown publicly and the near-miss must feel earned. Synonyms stop the "西红柿 vs 番茄" rage-quit for the mass audience. | Bot-only gate (no review): misses embarrassing neighbours. No synonyms (Contexto-style purity): unfriendly to casual players. kept-ideas-alt's "reject if bot > ~40 guesses" only: no lower bound, so some days would be trivial. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-10 | **Every share is a challenge** on the same puzzle (`c=<id>`). The card shows the step count, hints used and a temperature column chart, **never a word**. After the recipient finishes: side-by-side comparison and the sharer's guess route. No share grants anything. | One share type keeps it simple. Spoiler-free cards can go into groups that haven't played. The route reveal is a conversation starter. Article 3.4 forbids rewarding shares. | Separate result and challenge cards (点将台): two buttons for one intent. Showing words on the card: spoils the day. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-11 | **Archive + practice pool** for unlimited unranked play: every past daily (answer shown only if you finished it) and ≥ 300 practice puzzles. 再来一题 opens the newest unplayed past daily, else the next practice puzzle. | The "play again now" loop and the ad inventory. Practice covers launch day, when the archive is empty. | Archive only (kept-ideas): nothing to replay on day 1. Random unlimited words generated live: impossible without live rank computation and review. 30-day archive cap: no reason to limit; plays are tiny. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-12 | **Streak = consecutive published days with a solved ranked daily.** A ranked give-up resets it; unpublished days are skipped. No rewards, no freezes, no reminders. | Rewards solving (the skill) without pressure tactics; mirrors 点将台 PO-6. | Count any finished day (incl. give-up): weaker meaning. Streak rewards or freezes: login-reward pressure, rejected. |
| 2026-10-15 | PO-13 | **Rank shown exactly; temperature `T = round(100 × (1 − ln r / ln V))`** sets bar fill and chart height; six bands by rank (命中 1, 沸 2-10, 烫 11-100, 热 101-500, 温 501-2,000, 冷 2,001+) with band words and our thermometer palette (red = close, blue = far). | Rank is the clearest single number; the log-scaled temperature makes progress from 9,000 to 900 feel like progress. | 2026-10-15 | PO-14 | **Limits:** 40 guesses per minute; 600 guesses per puzzle. | Stops scripts from walking the whole vocabulary to find rank 1, while staying effectively unlimited for humans. | No cap: a script can solve any daily in ≤ 30,000 calls.
