# Research round 2026-10-15: new ideas

## Brief
The founder asked for a new list of WeChat mini game ideas under the same rules as the 2026-10-14 round. The list had to include at least one 末日生存 (doomsday survival) concept and at least one BitLife-related concept. Every idea is tested against constitution Article 3 (simple, addictive, social). The other rules: IAA only at launch, original IP, unique in China, no gambling-like mechanics or paid randomness, no anonymous friend-rating, no runtime generative AI, and content safe for review. Effort is credited for reuse of the 点将台 stack.

## Files
- `00-shortlist.md`: opportunity-analyst's scored shortlist. It has 11 candidates, a weighted score table, the top 5 in detail, the recommendation, the shared-engine view, content rules and the founder's in-app checks.
- `01-doomsday-survival.md`: 末日生存 saturation on WeChat, Western short-session references, gaps and compliance.
- `02-life-sim.md`: BitLife and 人生重开模拟器, China saturation, AIGC rules and the life-sim angles.
- `03-fresh-sweep.md`: 15 other short-session social formats (Contexto, Loldle, Which Year, Wavelength, Higher/Lower and more).

## Outcome

| Rank | Idea | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **同一场末日**, a daily shared-seed swipe survival game: "you survived N days" | **4.15** (recommended next build) |
| 2 | 猜将, a Loldle-style 三国 daily | 3.95 (as a 点将台 mode, not a new app) |
| 3 | 比一比, Higher/Lower streaks | 3.75 |
| 4 | 今日人生, a daily shared-seed life (BitLife-related) | 3.60 |
| 5 | 群聊避难所, a group co-op shelter | 3.60 |

- **Shared engine:** 同一场末日 and 今日人生 should share one daily shared-scenario choice engine and ship as two separate apps. 今日人生 then costs about 3-4 weeks.
- **Rejected:** 语义热冷 (fails Simple: typing, not one-thumb), 一日一年 and a standalone 末日人格卡 (both fail Addictive; the personality card becomes the onboarding of #1), and 搜刮突袭 (SLG-adjacent and hostile).

## Status
Awaiting the founder's choice (constitution Art. 2.1) and the in-app competitor checks in `00-shortlist.md` section 8. The most important check is to play 生存33天 and search WeChat for daily swipe survival games.
