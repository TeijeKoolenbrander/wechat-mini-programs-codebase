# Research round 2026-10-14: social mini games

## Brief
Find short (30 s to 3 min), addictive, social WeChat mini games that are unique in China, adapted from recent US hits, and assess whether porting 82-0 (the viral NBA roster-draft game at 82-0.com) is legal. Constraints: IAA only at launch, no third-party IP or real athletes, no gambling-like mechanics, MVP in 4-8 weeks.

## Files
- `00-shortlist.md`: opportunity-analyst's scored shortlist of 9 concepts, recommendation, 82-0 verdict and open questions for the founder.
- `01-us-source-games.md`: US/global short-session social casual games as a source pool, with 82-0 profiled in depth.
- `02-china-saturation.md`: China mini game market size, charts, saturated genres and gaps.
- `03-social-mechanics-monetization.md`: WeChat sharing/leaderboard mechanics, monetization benchmarks, case studies and effort.
- `04-legal-compliance.md`: IP, 版号, filing and entity requirements for adapting 82-0 and similar games.

## Outcome
- **Recommended: 点将台** (working title), an original 三国 draft-and-grade game using the 82-0 loop plus a daily shared seed and group leaderboards. Score **4.00** of 5.
- **Runner-up: 今日四组**, a Connections-style daily Chinese puzzle with group leaderboards. Score **3.80**; cheapest to build but weakest under IAA.
- **82-0 verdict:** no port (NBA name/likeness rights, copying risk), no licence now, build an original game inspired by the mechanic (not fictional basketball).
