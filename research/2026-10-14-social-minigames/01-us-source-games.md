# US/global short-session social casual games: source pool for WeChat mini game ideas

Date: 2026-10-14. Author: market-researcher. Scope: US/Western games popular in roughly the last 12-24 months. Facts carry a source URL. Inferences are labelled INFERENCE. Anything not found is marked "not found". Many figures are third-party estimates (FoxData, AppGoblin, AppMagic via press), not official.

Limitations: the search tooling returned thin results for several titles (Pips, Strands, Wavelength, GamePigeon, Discord Activities). Those entries have little or no traction data. I did not search Chinese-language sources for existing WeChat clones of these games, except one quick query on 82-0 that found nothing. That check is a priority follow-up.

## 1. Key findings

- **"82-0" is almost certainly 82-0.com, a viral NBA all-time roster-draft game.** It is by creator Roy Saar with Israeli studio PlayVault (publisher label Vaulty Studios Inc.). Each of 5 rounds you spin a random team and era, pick one player into a position slot, and the site grades the five-man lineup by simulated regular-season wins. A perfect 82-0 is the goal. It went viral in early June 2026 after Tyrese Haliburton posted his 77-5 result on June 3, 2026. See section 2.1 for sources and the ambiguity note.
- The 82-0 formula is spawning a genre: "random spin, draft, graded score, share the lineup". Clones and sibling titles include Ultimate 11 (soccer, same publisher), 8-0 (World Cup) and dozens of imitators. PlayVault says an NFL version and a league soccer version are in development.
- **Daily-puzzle games remain the largest proven habit loop in the West.** NYT Games players solved 11.2B puzzles in 2025: Wordle 4.2B, Connections 1.6B, Strands 1.5B (TODAY/NBC, 2025-12-29).
- **Hybrid-casual is the only mobile segment growing in revenue.** In-app revenue was up about 20% to about $4.2B in 2025 (Cinevva analysis of Sensor Tower/AppMagic data; secondary source).
- **Short-video-native "satisfying" physics games break out with zero paid UA.** Smash Fest! (Flow Games, Istanbul) reached the top 3 of the US iOS free games chart on 2026-06-15 with about 2.9M first-month downloads (FoxData estimate).
- **Block Blast!** (Hungry Studio) is the benchmark ad-supported casual puzzler. The company claims 70M DAU and 300M MAU at end-2025. Third-party AppMagic estimates are lower.
- **Roblox "brainrot" shows how fast fads decay.** Steal a Brainrot peaked at about 25M concurrent in Oct 2025 and a tracker now shows about 0.2M. Treat Roblox games as long-session, so they are a poor format fit.
- **Many viral Western daily and sports games are solo-play with a social share-out.** Examples are Wordle grids and 82-0 lineups. They have no real-time multiplayer. This maps well to WeChat sharing, because WeChat's social graph is stronger than the web's.
- INFERENCE: The best WeChat candidates combine (a) a draft/pick-and-grade loop, (b) a result card for sharing to group chats, (c) a daily seed for group-chat leaderboards. Fit with China needs a localized content pool (see risks on licensing and cultural fit).

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 "82-0": what it is

**Primary candidate (high confidence): 82-0.com, "82-0: Draft Legends" (web and iOS/Android).**
- Mechanics (ALLCITY, Tim Cato, 2026-06-16, https://allchgo.com/?p=144231): each round you spin for a team and an era, then pick one player from that team for your starting five. You can re-spin team and era once each. The site grades the squad on how many regular-season games it would win. Only a perfect 82-0 counts as an undefeated season.
- Further rules (via search summary of https://82-0-challenge.com/ and https://aftermath.site/82-0-free-basketball-game-browser.md): each player is placed at a position he actually played, and each of the five slots fills once. Classic mode shows stats. HoopIQ mode hides them. Note that "82-0-challenge.com" is a separate site and may be a clone rather than the original. I did not verify its ownership.
- Creator and owner: Roy Saar, a lifelong NBA fan. He partnered with PlayVault (Israel), whose CEO Bar Almog said Saar has joined the team. PlayVault's publishing arm is Vaulty Studios (https://allchgo.com/?p=144231). The App Store listing developer is Vaulty Studios Inc. (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/82-0-com/id6780195090).
- Virality: on 2026-06-03 Tyrese Haliburton posted he was "retiring" from the game. His LeBron/Kobe/Jordan/Bird/Moses lineup was graded 77-5 (https://allchgo.com/?p=144231; also https://clutchpoints.com/nba/indiana-pacers/pacers-news-tyrese-haliburton-retires-from-82-0-challenge-after-absurd-result). Aftermath (Luke Plunkett, 2026-06-03) said it "blew up online just this week" and that the game describes itself as from 2025.
- Traction: App Store #8 in Sports free chart, 4.8 rating from about 14K ratings, version 1.0.0 dated Jun 24 (so the app launched about three weeks after the viral moment). Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/82-0-com/id6780195090 (fetched 2026-10-14). The app was still updating, and the chart rank is a point-in-time reading. AppGoblin estimate: about 119K installs and about 45K MAU (https://appgoblin.info/apps/6780195090). This is a third-party estimate and likely understates the web traffic. No official web traffic numbers found.
- Monetization: the web version is free. An early version showed a Ko-fi donation pop-up for server costs (Aftermath). The iOS app has IAP coin and item packs from $2.99 to $19.99 (App Store listing). PlayVault said it plans deeper gamification and leaderboards.
- Siblings and clones: Ultimate 11 (soccer, Vaulty Studios, listed as first launched 2026-06-22 per a third-party aggregator, https://mwm.ai/apps/ultimate-11/6779740065; https://appgoblin.info/apps/6779740065). 8-0 (World Cup clone mentioned in the ALLCITY interview). PlayVault did not make the copies, per the ALLCITY interview.
- Other "82-0" candidates considered:
  - "82-0 Challenge" (82-0-challenge.com, web, with "One Franchise" and "X-0 Games" variants): same concept, probable clone or parallel site. Ownership not found.
  - "82-0 Mobile" (BlueStacks listing of com.DefaultCompany.Mobile82_0): drafts current players. Unclear link to the others.
  - The NBA fact that no team has gone 82-0 (best is 73 wins) is just the name's origin.
  - Confidence that the founder means 82-0.com is high, because it is the only one with major press and a celebrity moment in 2026. Please confirm with the founder.

### 2.2 Game pool (22 entries)

Format: core loop / session / social-viral mechanic / monetization / traction / IP owner.

**Draft-and-grade (the 82-0 family)**

1. **82-0.com** (see 2.1). Loop: spin team+era, draft 5 players, get a win-total grade. Session: about 2-4 minutes (INFERENCE from 5 picks; the interviewer said a first perfect-attempt took days, but a single run is short). Social: shareable lineup and grade, celebrity posts, comparison with friends on social media; leaderboards planned. Monetization: free web, IAP in app. Traction as above. IP: Vaulty Studios / PlayVault; uses real NBA player names (licensing status not found).
2. **Ultimate 11** (soccer, Vaulty Studios). Loop: random nation and era draw, draft an 11-man squad, formation, simulated tournament. Modes: Classic (stats shown) and Soccer IQ (hidden). Global leaderboard. Free. Traction: not found beyond listings. Sources: https://mwm.ai/apps/ultimate-11/6779740065, https://www.bluestacks.com/campaign/com.vaultystudios.ultimate11/en/. IP: Vaulty Studios.
3. **Immaculate Grid** (and Puckdoku, Football Grid etc.). Loop: 3x3 daily grid, name a player who fits both the row and column criteria. Session: about 3-5 minutes (INFERENCE). Social: shareable score, rarity score vs all players. Monetization: ads, possible subscription. Traction: acquired by Sports Reference (July 2023, about 200K weekday users then: https://frontofficesports.com/article/new-owner-of-immaculate-grid-baseball-game-eyes-football-other-sports/ - STALE, over 12 months). A Substack cites about 18.5M visits in one month (https://sportspundit.substack.com/p/immaculate-grid-a-not-so-trivial, date unverified). A game show adaptation is in development at Religion of Sports (https://awfulannouncing.com/tv/immaculate-grid-game-show-religion-sports.html). IP: Sports Reference.

**Daily puzzles**

4. **Wordle (NYT).** Loop: guess a 5-letter word in 6 tries, one puzzle a day. Session: 2-5 min. Social: emoji result grid pasted in chats, streaks. Monetization: NYT subscription funnel. Traction: 4.2B solves in 2025 (https://www.nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/hardest-wordle-connections-games-2025/6436464/, 2025-12-29). IP: The New York Times. Created in 2021, so the format is old, but the metrics are current.
5. **Connections (NYT).** Loop: group 16 words into 4 hidden categories. Session: 2-5 min. Social: color-block result grid. Traction: 1.6B solves in 2025 (same source). IP: NYT.
6. **Strands (NYT).** Loop: themed word search with a spangram. Traction: 1.5B solves in 2025 (same source). Social: result grid. IP: NYT.
7. **NYT Pips** (domino-matching puzzle). Traction: 612M solves in 2025 per the TODAY article (as reported in a search summary; not re-verified). Treated as a data point on new-format traction. IP: NYT.
8. **LinkedIn Games (Queens, Tango, Zip, Pinpoint, Crossclimb, Mini Sudoku, Wend).** Loop: daily logic puzzles. Session: 1-5 min. Social: results compared with first-degree connections (a professional graph). Traction: no usage data found. Queens puzzle number 814 on 2026-07-23 shows a long daily cadence (https://fandomwire.com/all-linkedin-games-solutions-for-today-july-23-2026/). Launch timeline: https://jenfoley.substack.com/p/linkedin-games-edition. IP: LinkedIn/Microsoft. Relevant as a precedent for a "friends-only" leaderboard inside an existing graph, which resembles WeChat.
9. **Bracket City** (The Atlantic). Loop: nested "exploded crossword" clues. Session: about 5-10 min (INFERENCE). Created by Ben Gross, licensed to The Atlantic June 2025 (https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/06/wordle-who-the-atlantic-launches-a-suite-of-new-daily-puzzles-and-games/). Traction numbers: not found. IP: The Atlantic / creator.
10. **Worldle / Globle / Capitalle and other geography dailies.** Loop: guess a country by silhouette or distance hints. Social: result grid. Traction: no 2026 figures found. https://www.capitalle.app/blog/worldle-alternatives. IP: various indies. Note that the daily-clone space is crowded, so differentiation is hard.
11. **Daily Red** (unofficial Pokemon daily). Loop: same six random level-100 Pokemon for everyone, beat Red once a day, with streak and spoiler-free results grid. Reported 2026-10-12 by Dexerto (https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/this-daily-pokemon-wordle-game-lets-you-fight-red-with-a-random-team-every-day-3418266/). Held Games, 2026-10-12, says the original site went offline and the repo was archived on 2026-10-11 (https://heldgames.com/guides/daily-red-pokemon-wordle-handhelds). Shows the IP takedown risk of fan-IP games. Traction: not found.

**Hybrid-casual / hyper-casual mobile**

12. **Block Blast!** (Hungry Studio). Loop: place 3 shapes on an 8x8 grid to clear lines. Session: 1-5 min. Social: weak (personal best). Monetization: ads, negligible IAP (https://mobilegamer.biz/hungry-studios-hit-puzzler-block-blast-is-heading-to-apple-arcade/). Traction: company claims 70M DAU / 300M MAU as of Dec 2025 (https://press.energydaily.co.kr/newsRead.php?no=1026753). AppMagic-based estimates are lower (https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast). #1 on both stores in Aug 2026 (about 6M App Store and about 19M Google Play monthly downloads, FoxData: https://foxdata.com/ru/blogs/global-mobile-game-rankings-august-2026-top-15-downloads-revenue/). Apple's most-downloaded free iPhone game of 2025 (GSMArena/Apple). IP: Hungry Studio. Chinese outlet reports of a Google Play removal are unconfirmed.
13. **Smash Fest!** (Flow Games). Loop: aim a cannon, smash stacked jars/crates with limited ammo. Session: short levels (no figure given). No built-in share feature. Growth came from store conversion and word of mouth; ads were playable-only. FoxData: top 3 US iOS from 2026-06-15, about 2.9M downloads in the first month, monthly revenue about $60K, US share 66.6% (https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/how-smash-fest-hit-1-on-the-us-app-store-with-zero-paid-uaand-what-it-means-for-hybrid-casual/, 2026-07-16). It was removed from Google Play in late July (FoxData Aug report). 36Kr traces the mechanic to Toon Blast's "Cannon Fest" mode (https://www.36kr.com/p/3878540336279809). Clone wave: Royal Smash etc. (LinkedIn claim, weak). IP: Flow Games.
14. **Bus Traffic Fever!** (GOODROID) and the arrow/jam puzzle cluster (Arrows-Puzzle Escape, Arrow Puzzle). Loop: tap vehicles/arrows to clear a jammed board. Session: 30-90 s per level (INFERENCE). Traction: iOS #15 to #8 in August 2026, downloads up 27%, boosted by short-video ads (FoxData Aug report). Social: none. Relevant because this genre is already common in China mini games (INFERENCE; not verified).
15. **Pizza Ready!** (Supercent / Molip, Korea). Idle cooking. #2 on Google Play, Aug 2026, about 18M downloads (FoxData). Over 100M lifetime downloads per a Turkish outlet citing AppMagic (https://mobidictum.com/tr/supercent-pizza-ready-100-milyon-indirmeye-ulasti/). Idle, no social mechanic. Not a core candidate.
16. **Annoying Uncle Punch Game.** New hyper-casual prank-humor entrant, #15 on Google Play in Aug 2026 (FoxData Aug report). No other data found.

21. **Messenger (Abeto)** free browser game. About 30 min total. Went viral in Oct 2025 (https://boingboing.net/2025/10/27/messenger-is-the-coolest-browser-game-ive-ever-played.html, https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250929-messenger-browser-game). ## 3. Mapping to WeChat social features

Everything below is INFERENCE unless a fact is cited.

| Mechanic from source games | WeChat feature |
|---|---|
| Shareable result card (Wordle grid, 82-0 lineup + grade, Immaculate Grid score) | Share to chat/group with a dynamic card (onShareAppMessage); share to Moments is available for mini games; generate an image card with `wx.shareAppMessage` imageUrl |
| Daily seed, same puzzle for everyone (Wordle, Daily Red, LinkedIn) | One cloud-seeded daily puzzle; group chat players compare same-day results; group-ranking via shareTicket/group openGId (check current API availability) |
| Friends-only leaderboard (LinkedIn connections, Snap friends) | Friend relationship chain via open data domain (开放数据域) leaderboard, which needs a minimal in-game rank view |
| Async head-to-head (GamePigeon) | Challenge link: friend opens the same seed, compares scores asynchronously |
| Streaks, "retire from the game" bragging (82-0, Haliburton) | Streak badge plus share cards; streak-saving by watching a rewarded video |
| Satisfying visual loops that cut well as clips (Smash Fest!) | Short video (视频号) UA and organic clips; Smash Fest's playable-only ads suggest very low content-production cost |
| Celebrity or fan-community moments (Haliburton post) | Chinese equivalents: sports stars and KOLs on 视频号 and Douyin (a content-seeding plan, not a game mechanic) |

Platform facts (WeChat group sharing, open data domain, 视频号) should be verified against developers.weixin.qq.com before specs; I did not verify current API details in this research pass.

## 4. Opportunities (candidate ideas with signal)

1. **"Draft and grade" with a China-relevant pool** (e.g., football legends via the World Cup/Chinese Super League, NBA stars popular in China, or non-sports pools like Chinese pop songs, TV drama casts, historical figures). Signal: 82-0 viral in June 2026, an iOS app within 3 weeks, siblings and dozens of clones. A group-chat "who built the best team" comparison fits WeChat well.
2. **Daily category-guessing puzzle in Chinese** (Connections/Strands-style using idioms, 成语, characters, regional food). Signal: Connections 1.6B and Strands 1.5B solves in 2025. Chinese-character puzzle formats are inherently different from Latin word games. Whether a similar daily shareable habit exists in China should be checked against 汉兜/成语 games.
3. **Physics-smash short-level game cut for 视频号 clips.** Signal: Smash Fest! zero paid UA, top 3 US iOS, and a mechanic borrowed from Toon Blast. Risk: fast clone wave and short life.
4. **Async 1v1 challenge game launched from a chat card** (GamePigeon-style). Signal: GamePigeon's iMessage model, Snap Games and Discord Activities all treat the chat as the lobby. Weak traction data in this pass.
5. **Jam/arrow puzzle with a friends-ranking layer.** Signal: arrow and bus jam titles climbing the Aug 2026 charts. Likely saturated in China already (not verified).

## 5. Risks, saturation, policy

- **Real-person likeness and league IP.** 82-0 uses real NBA players. In China, name and portrait use, NBA IP and WeChat's review of sports content are uncertain. Licensing status of the original game was not found. A fictionalized or public-domain content pool lowers risk.
- **Fan-IP takedowns.** Daily Red's site went offline and its repo was archived on 2026-10-11 (Held Games). Avoid Pokemon/anime IP.
- **WeChat mini game licensing (版号/备案).** Not researched in this pass. Casual and puzzle IAA games often use a lighter registration path, but this must be verified with the policy research track before committing.
- **Fads decay fast.** Steal a Brainrot fell about 99% from peak; Smash Fest! downloads fell about 18% in one month and it left Google Play.
- **Clone saturation.** The 82-0 format already has dozens of clones in the West. In China, copying within weeks is common (INFERENCE), so speed and a distinctive content hook matter.
- **Daily-puzzle monetization.** Most daily games are free with ads or a subscription funnel (NYT, Atlantic). The standalone indie economics are unproven. In WeChat the likely model is rewarded-video ads (IAA). That fits streak-saving or hint mechanics. eCPM data was not researched here.
- **Data quality.** FoxData, AppGoblin and many "download" numbers are estimates. Some search summaries I relied on were secondhand. Verify before using figures in a business case.
